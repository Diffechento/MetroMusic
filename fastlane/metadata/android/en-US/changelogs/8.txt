The player can show the song's words in place of the cover, timed words following the music. Playlists are .m3u files in Music/Playlists, with import, export and adding a whole album at once. Shuffle rearranges the queue; play next puts it next. Long tracks resume where you left them. Years are read from FLAC, Ogg and ID3v2.4 tags. Tap the artist or album in the player to open its page.
